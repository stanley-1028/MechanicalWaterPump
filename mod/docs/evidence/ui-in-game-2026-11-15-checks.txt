Ordering note (honest)
----------------------
ToolOrder 53/54/55/56 places our tiers right after the vanilla mechanical pumps (50-52) and before
the centrifuge (60). On this heavily modded install some other water mods also use 53-56, so their
buildings interleave between our Mk I/II/III/IV icons; all four are nevertheless in the vanilla
Water group, in Mk I -> Mk IV order. On a vanilla-only install they appear consecutively right
after the vanilla mechanical pumps.

Synthetic input note (update of the 0.2.4 finding)
-------------------------------------------------
The 0.2.4 round could not click because the game was running in the DSH sandbox (low-integrity /
restricted desktop) and OpenInputDesktop was denied. In this round the game ran with normal user
integrity (danger-full-access session) and synthetic input works: SetCursorPos + mouse_event
(click) and injected relative mouse moves (hover) reach the game window while it is foreground.
That is how the Water group was opened and the tooltips were captured.
