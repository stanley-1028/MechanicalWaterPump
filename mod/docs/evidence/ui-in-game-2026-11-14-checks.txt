Limitation of this environment (honest)
---------------------------------------
Synthetic mouse/keyboard input is not available on this machine: the harness probe
(Test-InputInjection.ps1) reports SetCursorPos/mouse_event/SendInput/keybd_event success but
`sendinput_return=3, form_clicks=0, form_keys=0`, and OpenInputDesktop fails with access denied
(the same environment fact recorded by the sibling HDBF project, VERIFICATION 16.2). Therefore the
two click-driven steps could not be automated in this round:
  1. clicking the MWP group to expand it (expand/collapse behaviour is the vanilla
     BlockObjectToolGroupButton code path, IL-verified in docs/api/ui/toolgroup-facts.md §b.8), and
  2. selecting a pump to open its entity panel (the panel's visual recipe is verified offline
     against the vanilla UXML/USS in docs/api/ui/panel-vanilla-facts.md and MWP.UI/README.md).
Both remain manual checks in VERIFICATION.md (T14-type UI pass); no claim of a Pass is made for
them here. No workaround that bypasses the environment restriction was used.
